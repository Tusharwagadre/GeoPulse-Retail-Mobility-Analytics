# Analysis Methodology

This is a proposed workflow. Adapt it to the official project brief and actual dataset.

1. **Understand the brief:** confirm the business question, scope, deliverables, and restrictions.
2. **Inventory the data:** record source, schema, grain, date range, and geographic coverage.
3. **Profile quality:** inspect missingness, duplicates, types, invalid values, and outliers.
4. **Prepare data:** apply documented, reproducible transformations without overwriting raw data.
5. **Explore:** investigate only patterns supported by available fields.
6. **Define KPIs:** agree on definitions, numerator, denominator, filters, and time window.
7. **Visualize:** select charts/maps that answer the agreed questions.
8. **Validate:** reconcile totals, test transformations, and review interpretations.
9. **Report:** distinguish observations from hypotheses and recommendations.
